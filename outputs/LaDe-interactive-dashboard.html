Interpretation notes

Use the dashboard to find questions for operations teams, not to claim causes.

  • Do not compare an all-period city P90 by averaging monthly or hourly P90 values.
  • Long intervals do not by themselves prove a missed promise; the dataset has no promised delivery deadline or SLA field.
  • Timestamp parsing uses a provisional 2022 year and no timezone. Negative or out-of-window intervals require source clarification.
  • The source covers five cities in China and a historical period. Do not generalize the result to present-day Indian delivery operations.
